# earnings-call-tone
this is a project to develop an ai that will determine the tone of the board on an earnings call and identify if that will create a swing in a stock

I hope to create a machine that will analyze the tone of the boards as well as the sentiment the expressed and then the machine will make a prediction of short mid and long term movement based on that by the end i hope to have a conclusion on whether or not the tone of the voices of the board has anything to do with how the stock may perfrom in the interim
